# On call

## Rotation
On call rotates every Monday at 10:00 local time. The outgoing engineer hands over open incidents in the on call channel before the rotation.

## Paging
A page must be acknowledged within 5 minutes. If it is not, it escalates to the secondary and then to the engineering manager after 15 minutes.

## Compensation
Each week on call adds one day off, taken within the next month.
